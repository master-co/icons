## Referenced work \{#sources\}

Some drawings were reconstructed with reference to Hugeicons Free or Lucide. Their applicable MIT or ISC notices are retained in the package, SVGs and download archives. Read the [third-party notices](/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for the exact attribution.

The independent review tool also includes upstream comparison drawings. Those research assets are separate from the Master Icons package and website; their original licenses remain with the research files.
