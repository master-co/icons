Master Icons is open source under the [MIT license](/LICENSE.txt). Preserve applicable copyright and license notices when redistributing the icons.

## Brand icons \{#brands\}

The `brand-` collection includes Simple Icons, official brand assets, the Master mark, and a documented Dcard raster-to-vector trace. Artwork licenses are separate from the code license: Vue retains CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 with additional permissions; JavaScript and GitHub Copilot retain upstream MIT notices; Microsoft and OpenAI assets retain their own terms. Source-specific notices are preserved in the package and downloads, including `licenses/brand-additions.txt`. Dcard uses an archived app mark; its original guide specifies a 48px minimum, so the smaller exports are optical previews rather than a claim of guideline compliance. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners; the code license does not grant trademark rights. See the [third-party notices](/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for individual sources and versions.

## Referenced work \{#sources\}

Some drawings were reconstructed with reference to Hugeicons Free or Lucide. Their applicable MIT or ISC notices are retained in the package, SVGs and download archives. Read the [third-party notices](/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for the exact attribution.

The independent review tool also includes upstream comparison drawings. Those research assets are separate from the Master Icons package and website; their original licenses remain with the research files.
