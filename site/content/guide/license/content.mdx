## Master Icons \{#master\}

Master Icons is open source under the [MIT license](/LICENSE.txt). Preserve applicable copyright and license notices when redistributing the icons.

## Brand icons \{#brands\}

The `brand-` collection includes Simple Icons, official brand assets, the Master mark, and a documented Dcard raster-to-vector trace. Artwork licenses are separate from the code license: Vue retains CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0 with additional permissions; JavaScript and GitHub Copilot retain upstream MIT notices; Microsoft and OpenAI assets retain their own terms. Source-specific notices are preserved in the package and downloads, including `licenses/brand-additions.txt`. Dcard uses an archived app mark; its original guide specifies a 48 px minimum, so the smaller exports are optical previews rather than a claim of guideline compliance. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners; the code license does not grant trademark rights. See the [third-party notices](/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for individual sources and versions.

## Adapted work \{#sources\}

Some drawings adapt the construction of other open-source icon sets, including Lucide, Tabler, Phosphor, Hugeicons Free, Iconoir and Material Design Icons. Each adapted icon keeps its upstream MIT, ISC or Apache 2.0 notice in its SVG file, the package and the download archives. Read the [third-party notices](/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for the exact source of every icon.

The `teapot` drawing adapts the lid of an OpenMoji emoji. That drawing and its modifications are licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0, not the MIT license; its SVG and the package include the complete notice.

The independent review tool also includes upstream comparison drawings. Those research assets are separate from the Master Icons package and website; their original licenses remain with the research files.
