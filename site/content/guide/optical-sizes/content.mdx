## Optical sizes \{#sizes\}

Each icon has drawings for 16, 20 and 24 pixels. Small drawings can simplify dense details; they are not merely scaled versions of the largest drawing.

The default display size is 24. Automatic optical selection chooses the nearest master; ties at 18 and 22 choose the larger one. CSS width and height do not trigger optical selection. Prefer native 16, 20 or 24 pixels for the intended result.

Start at 24 px for a general interface, and choose 16 or 20 px for a dense one, such as a toolbar or a table. Larger display sizes scale the 24 px drawing.

## Corner styles \{#corners\}

Choose `sharp`, `soft` or `rounded`. The default is `soft`. These styles adjust geometry and internal spacing, not only SVG line joins. Natural curves and deliberately flat details can remain unchanged across styles.

Compare all nine masters in an icon's detail dialog in the [collection](/icons). To change line weight as well, see [stroke width](/guide/stroke-width).
